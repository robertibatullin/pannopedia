Districts of South Vronovica

The South, or New Vronovica, is situated on the right bank of South Drava. The oldest connection to the northern part of the city, described in a separate article, was the Turkish bridge (Turski most), leading to the island fortress. Initially this part of the city — so-called Transdravian, or Zadravska — comprised the Fifth District lying along the river bank. The Gowbka regime added the Sixth and Seventh Districts to the south, both comprising Sockostur.
