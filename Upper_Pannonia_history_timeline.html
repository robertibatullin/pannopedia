Year Event
mid 9th c. Cyril and Methodius' mission to Great Moravia
early 10th c. Dissolution of Great Moravia; part of the Slavic population pushed south by the Hungarian invasion
10th–16th c. Upper Pannonian Slavic principalities, mostly Hungarian vassals
1040 St. Tibor established an episcopal seat in Bregovo, imposing the Latin liturgy across the country
1529 Ottoman Turks seized Upper Pannonia; Bregovo pillaged and burned
1534 Turks built Kuzgun Qalesi fortress, the future Vronovica
1709 Austrians recaptured Upper Pannonia
1711 UP organized as a district of the Austrian Military Frontier, named Generalat Oberpannonien
1712–1720 Kuzgun Qalesi massively rebuilt into a Vauban star fortress, renamed Rabenburg (a.k.a. Slavic Vronovica, a.k.a. Hungarian Hollófészek)
1755 St. Tibor Cathedral consecrated in Vronovica, becoming the new UP episcopal seat
1781 Enjo Zslutovody installed a Freemasonic lodge "Pannonia Prima", including Slavic National Enlightenment enthusiasts, later branched into multiple cultural and intellectual associations
late 18th c. – mid 19th c. Slavic National Enlightenment emerged, opposing Hungarian cultural hegemony
1848–1849 UP drawn into the Hungarian Revolution: supported by Zgurians but opposed by Deglians and UP nobility, who were Habsburg-leaning
1850 Vienna granted limited autonomy to UP as Banat Oberpannonien
1867 Under the Austro-Hungarian Compromise, UP retained its autonomy within the Kingdom of Hungary
1903 Etelka Baumgarten born
1914 Severyn Gowbka born
1918 Fall of the Austro-Hungarian Empire; UP regional parliament proclaimed independence (First Republic)
1922 Plebiscite in UP: most Zgurians voted for independence, most Deglians for union with Yugoslavia; pro-independence vote won (ca. 60%)
1931 Field Marshal Drogan Glovacz seized dictatorial power as the leader of the UP National Empowerment Movement
1932 New Vronovica Cathedral of SS. Cyril and Methodius consecrated
1933 Mass executions of Communists, Anarchists and Social Democrats; Severyn Gowbka fled to Moscow
1933 Lukas Rudecki, bishop ov Vronovica, elevated to Cardinal
1934 Drogan Glovacz enthroned as King Drogan I
1936 Adelj, wife of King Drogan, enthroned as Queen Adele and proclaimed heiress to the UP throne
1939 Ljubodar Vloszek born
1940 Hungary invaded and annexed UP; King Drogan assassinated; Queen Adele fled to England and established a government in exile
1941 Ljudmila, posthumous daughter of King Drogan, born in London
1942 Pro-Soviet UP National Liberation Committee, headed by Gowbka, and the UP People's Liberation Army established in Moscow
1944 UP liberated by the Red Army offensive; Gowbka appointed head of a Soviet-backed UP government; 17 November - Liberation Day
1945 At the Yalta Conference, Stalin demanded that Churchill cease support for Queen Adele's government in exile
1945 People's Republic of UP proclaimed
1948 Cardinal Rudecki forced to emigrate
1945–1949 Gowbka consolidated a Communist dictatorial regime
1952 Queen Adele poisoned in London, presumably by NOON agents; the 11-year-old Princess Ljudmila proclaimed Queen in exile by her supporters and transferred to the US for safety
1956 Gowbka began reorienting his alliance from the USSR towards Yugoslavia
1966 Gowbka broke with Yugoslavia
1967 Production of Drava cars began
1989 Gowbka died of a heart attack
1990 Fall of the Communist regime in UP; new democratic republic proclaimed (Third Republic); Deglian activists demanded national autonomy, then independence
1991 Third Republic government paralyzed amid growing Zguro-Deglian polarization and violence
1992 Zguro-Deglian negotiations broke down; the Third Republic collapsed; full-scale civil war erupted between Zgurians and Deglians
1994 Ceasefire and temporary agreement achieved through the mediation of Queen-in-exile Ljudmila; the Second Kingdom proclaimed; Queen Ljudmila assassinated during enthronement; ceasefire broken; civil war resumed
1995 War halted by UN peacekeepers; Lausanne Agreements: UP split into two de facto states separated by a neutral zone, with a UN High Commissioner overseeing and mediating until a legitimate government is established
1996 Ljubodar Vloszek elected President of Zgurian Republic
1999 Coup d'etat in Deglian Republic; the Secret Control Committee, an anonymous military junta, seized power. Permanent martial law in Deglian Vronovica
2000-2005 Vloszek family consolidated power in Zgurian republic
Present Lausanne Agreements in effect: Zgurian-Deglian negotiations in permanent deadlock; no legitimate government established; UN High Commissioner remains the only internationally recognized authority
