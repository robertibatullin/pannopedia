Pannonian passport scam - DRAFT

If you were lucky enough to be born a citizen of a developed country, you've probably never seen internet ads promoting Zgurian or Deglian citizenship. The target audience is refugees, illegal immigrants, and citizens of less lucky countries. These services advertise a fully legal purchase of a European country citizenship for the equivalent of $1,000–$2,000, paid in crypto, immediately, entirely online, with no formalities, and with a passport delivered anywhere in the world within two weeks. Some firms with physical offices even offer a biometry option.

This is all true. Technically, it isn't a scam — you get exactly what you pay for. These passports are legally issued by Zgurian Republic (ZR) or Deglian Republic (DR) authorities, the firms are authorized, and the purchase genuinely makes you a DR or ZR citizen. The problem is that this citizenship is absolutely useless for any reasonable purpose, or even worse.

[EDIT] ZR and DR are two rivalling de-facto states of the Former Upper Pannonia not officially recognized by any other state except Transnistria and Tuvalu. They even don't recognize each other. It means that their passports are almost nowhere considered valid travel documents. You can't receive any visa for a DR or ZR passport, and cannot even entry any visa-free country with it. In the best case, a border control officer treats it as a funny booklet, and in the worst case, as a travel document forgery. You hardly can predict the outcome.

[EDIT] The only territory which DR or ZR passport makes you eligible to enter, is the Former Upper Pannonia itself. Different entry options are described below.

Entry through the Neutral Zone

[EDIT] By Lausanne agreements, UN authorities (represented, in this case, as border control officers in Vronovica International Airport) may not prevent DR or ZR citizens either to leave FUP or to return to FUP. The border control is instructed to interpret it literally and exclusively. DR or ZR passport make you elugible to return to FUP - not to enter it for the first time. If your passport doesn't have stamps confirming departure from FUP through the Vronovica International Airport, you will be not let into the country.

[EDIT] This means that the UN administration, in fact, does not recognize mail-order ZR and DR passports. No exceptions are recorded so far.

Entry into Zgurian Republic by land

[EDIT] ZR has land border crossings with Austria, Hungary, Slovenia, and Croatia. They are out of the UN administration control. The limitations described above don't apply. You can enter ZR by land with you Zgurian passport (but of course not Deglian one).

[EDIT] There is a caveat, though. To enter ZR by land, you should start from one of the neighbouring countries. They all are in the Schengen zone. If you have already managed to arrive into the Schengen zone, you usually have better options than settling in ZR. There are few jobs and even fewer legal business opportunities. And for a touristic visit, the passport cost seems overpriced.

[EDIT] But otherwise, this option works. If you are really determined to visit ZR for whatever reason, purchasing its passport may be the fastest option, though not the cheapest one.

Entry into Deglian Republic by land

[EDIT] Just don't do this. DR has border crossings with Romania, Serbia and Croatia. Of these countries, Serbia is not a Schengen state and relatively easy to enter. You can arrive into Belgrade or Novi Sad with your primary passport, go to the Degilan border, show a Deglian officer your Deglian passport (of course not Zgurian one), and you are in. Very easy. But don't do this.

[EDIT] Passport-selling firms never tell you, that since 1999 DR is under permanent martial law, which means Deglian citizens cannot leave the country without a special permission. Conditions of obtaining such a permissions are not prohibitive but untransparent and unpredictable. Don't think you can easily bribe a Deglian official. They usually don't trust foreigners and are hesitant to receive bribes from people they don't know. Since 2003, bribery in DR is a capital offence for both parties involved.

[EDIT] By Lausanne agreements, ZR and DR are not allowed to have conscription armies. DR under post-1999 military regime developed a workaround: the so-called Civilian Defence Service which is mandatory for all adult male citizens and is essentially a military service, but without lethal weapons. This is not a formality. If you enter DR with a DR passport being an adult male, you will be inevitably conscripted into the Civilian Defence Service and obliged to pass a 6 months drill, which is harsh. Avoiding conscription is legally treated as desertership which is a capital offence.

[EDIT] This explains the fact that DR passports are usually 2 to 3 times cheaper that the ZR ones. Anyway, neither worth their price. If you want to visit FUP as a tourist, do it with your primary passport as descibed in a separate guide. If you plan for whatever reason settle in FUP permanently, think twice.
