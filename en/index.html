This website is devoted to Former Upper Pannonia, a fictional Eastern European country. It's mildly satirical, poking fun at Eastern European nationalism and Western stereotypes about Eastern Europe alike. But the parody never tips into absurdity: the author takes worldbuilding seriously and tries to stay within the bounds of plausibility.

Some sources of inspiration:

  • The Ruritania trope in general

  • Cinema of the former Yugoslavia

  • Ádám Bodor, The Sinistra Zone

  • Italo Calvino, If on a winter's night a traveler (Cimmeria and Cimbria chapters)

  • The game Papers, Please

Almost all illustrations are AI-generated (Grok Imagine, Nano Banana), and many have since been edited by hand. The texts are mostly written by hand, then translated and edited with AI assistance (Gemini, Claude). In some articles, particularly those on ecosystems, AI was used for fact-generation, but in every case the ideas originated in the author's own biological brain, and every text went through manual editing, sometimes amounting to a full rewrite.

Start with the Former Upper Pannonia page or the list of categories.
