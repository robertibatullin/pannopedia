Districts of North Vronovica

General layout

The layout of the North, or Old Vronovica, is governed by the island fortress. The fortress sits on the bank of the South Drava; the Fortress Canal (Grodski ruv) encloses it from the north, turning the castle precinct into an artificial island. Three gated bridges project the principal avenues outward from the fortress in a Y-configuration that continues to organize the modern city.

From the Viennese bridge (Beczki most), Slavic avenue (Slavjanska jezda) runs north-west as the main axis of the Zgurian zone. From the Buda bridge (Budajski most), Jezda Enja Zslutovody extends north-east through the Neutral Zone. From the Turkish bridge (Turski most), Independence avenue (Jezda Nepodstoja) crosses the South Drava southward, leading to the South, or New, or Transdravian Vronovica, described in a separate article.

Since the late Habsburg period this part of the city has been divided into four enumerated districts. The First District comprises the fortress and the blocks adjacent to the Bridged Ring. The Second covers the north-east sector along Enjo Zslutovody avenue, including the old city cemetery to its south. The Third occupies the north-west sector around Slavjanska jezda, and the Fourth fills the northern sector between the Second and Third.
